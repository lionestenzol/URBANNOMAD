Welcome to Urban Nomad

Your all-in-one companion for vehicle dwelling. This guide explains every feature so you can get the most out of the app.

Tap any section below to expand it.

Getting Started

Urban Nomad works entirely offline and stores all data on your device. No account or internet connection required.

  • Allow location access when prompted — this powers zone logging, resource finding, and spot notes.
  • Use the tabs at the top to navigate between features.
  • Dark mode — tap the moon icon in the header to switch themes (easier on the eyes at night).
  • Back up your data regularly using Settings > Export Backup.
Zones Tab

What it does: Track where you park and sleep. Build a personal log of locations you've visited.

  • Log a zone — type a name or tap one from the Zone Cheat Sheet, then hit "Log".
  • Zone Cheat Sheet — common urban nomad parking spots rated by stealth level (more stars = more discreet).
  • Visit Log — your history of logged zones with timestamps and GPS coordinates.
Vehicle Tab

What it does: Track fuel usage and calculate your miles per gallon (MPG).

  • Log fuel — enter your current odometer reading and gallons added, then tap "Log Fuel & Mileage".
  • MPG calculation — after your second entry, the app auto-calculates MPG based on miles driven between fill-ups.
  • Odometer validation — the app prevents logging a mileage lower than your last entry to keep data accurate.

Tip: Log every fill-up for the most accurate MPG tracking.

Gear Tab

What it does: A reference list of essential gear for vehicle dwelling.

  • Nomad Gear Kit — curated items like blackout curtains, power inverters, and USB fans.
  • Essential Checklist — grouped into Stealth Parking, Power System, and Comfort categories.

Use this as a shopping/packing checklist when outfitting your vehicle.

Resources Tab

What it does: Find nearby showers, restrooms, WiFi, and parking — plus save your own spots.

  • Find Resources — tap a category (Showers, Restrooms, WiFi, Parking) to see nearby options sorted by distance.
  • Refresh Location — update your GPS position for more accurate results.
  • Add Resource — save a new resource with name, type, coordinates, cost, and hours. Use "Use My Location" to auto-fill GPS.
  • My Resources — view and manage resources you've added.
  • My Spot Notes — tag locations as safe, unsafe, police activity, etc. These are private notes stored only on your device.
Plan Tab

What it does: Calculate how long your money will last for food and fuel.

  • Enter your budget — money available, daily food cost, gas price per gallon, and your vehicle's MPG.
  • Food/Fuel slider — drag to adjust how much of your budget goes to food vs. fuel (default 70/30).
  • Auto MPG — if you've logged vehicle data, MPG is pre-filled automatically.
  • Results — shows days of food, miles of range, and recommendations based on your situation.

Check the Quick Reference below the planner for budget meal ideas and free resource tips.

Settings Tab

What it does: Back up and restore all your app data.

  • Export Backup — downloads a JSON file with all your zones, vehicle logs, resources, and spot notes.
  • Import Backup — upload a previously exported file to restore your data. This replaces all current data.

Back up regularly, especially before clearing your browser data.

Safety Tips
  • Rotate spots — don't park in the same place every night. Use 3-4 spots in rotation.
  • Stay low-key — arrive late, leave early. Avoid running lights or making noise.
  • Know local laws — overnight parking rules vary by city. Check before settling in.
  • Have an exit plan — always know your route out if you need to leave quickly.
  • Trust your instincts — if a spot feels wrong, move. Your safety comes first.
  • Keep your vehicle ready — maintain enough fuel to relocate at any time.
FAQ

Is my data shared or uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything is stored locally on your device using your browser's storage. Nothing leaves your phone or computer.

Will I lose my data if I clear my browser?
Yes. Use Settings > Export Backup to save a copy before clearing browser data.

Does this work offline?
Yes. The app is a Progressive Web App (PWA) and works without an internet connection after the first load.

How do I install it on my phone?
Open the app in your mobile browser, then tap "Add to Home Screen" (Safari) or the install prompt (Chrome).

Why does it ask for my location?
Location is used to log GPS coordinates with your zones, find nearby resources, and tag spot notes. You can deny it — the app still works, just without location features.
